01 / THE BIG PICTURE

WHY THE NORTHEAST MATTERS

Security is more than the absence of conflict.

India's Northeast occupies a strategically important position, sharing international borders and connecting India with Southeast Asia.

Its mountainous terrain, border environment, connectivity, communities and development challenges create a security landscape where preparedness and stability are closely linked.

This publication examines the Indian Army as a national institution contributing to preparedness, operational capability, resilience, community engagement and national integration.

09 / CASE STUDIES

FROM EVENT TO IMPACT

Challenge → Response → Outcome → Significance

CASE 01

Cross-Border Drug Trafficking

Challenge

Illicit narcotics movement through the Northeast.

Response

DRI-led action supported by Assam Rifles personnel.

Outcome

231.8 kg of suspected methamphetamine was seized.

Why it matters

Demonstrates coordinated action against organised trafficking.

CASE 02

Women's Literacy & Empowerment

Challenge

Expanding educational opportunities within communities.

Response

Assam Rifles supported a literacy and empowerment initiative.

Outcome

50 students participated in the initiative.

Why it matters

Highlights the relationship between social development and community resilience.

CASE 03

Community Cleanliness

Challenge

Improving awareness around sanitation and civic responsibility.

Response

Army personnel participated in Swachhata Hi Seva activities.

Outcome

Community-oriented cleanliness activities were conducted in Agartala.

Why it matters

Demonstrates how outreach can complement wider institutional roles.
